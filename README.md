# Important for editing
You may replace (delete and re-upload new under the EXACT same name) `image.png` with a relevant image of your choice that fits the 15:13 aspect ratio of the image space on the "FEATURED" section of the Canterlands title screen (other aspect ratios *technically work*, but will stetch). `image.png` is automatically given rounded corners on the client by FancyMenu. You may also type in basic markdown for `description.md` and `title.md` and it should be parsed on the client.

I would also personally recommend fading your image of choice out to the left into a `#00000000` (transparent) or `#E9D6BF` (widget background colour) for a nicer, more intended look- it is up to you though and it should still look good without.

For clients to see a new, refreshed "FEATURED" section on their main menu, it may require a client restart or ~1 hour wait.

There is no fallback for the marked `image.png`, `title.md` or `description.md` files not being present or reachable at a specific moment in time, you may see artifact text, empty boxes or just nothing in those cases.
